Good to know before you buy

Deployment is quoted separately

The licence price doesn't include deployment and implementation. We quote that based on your location. If your PC needs preparing first, for example drivers, an operating system or supporting software, the quote includes those costs too.

Printers and other hardware

NTmart Go works with all popular receipt printers, including Epson, EPOS and Xprinter. Other hardware, such as barcode scanners and weighing machines, needs further configuration. Tell us what you use when you ask for a quote.

Lifetime licence

Pay once for the licence and use NTmart Go for life.

Yearly maintenance

33% of your licence price, paid each year. It covers training, visits for diagnostics, and custom upgrades.

Runs on modest PCs

Both plans run smoothly on older and low-spec computers.
