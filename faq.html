FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us.

General

What is NTmart Go?

A simple point of sale for Windows. It covers sales, invoicing, stock, buying, accounts and reports.

Who is it for?

Businesses that want a simple, dependable system: open it up, make sales, create invoices and quotes, manage stock and track the accounts. If you don't need fancy screens or lots of extra features, NTmart Go is for you.

How is it different from NTmart POS?

NTmart Go is the simpler, lighter option. It's a native Windows program that focuses on the everyday essentials, works 100% offline and runs well on modest hardware. It also works with your existing NTmart data.

What can it do?

Sales with receipts, invoices and quotes, client accounts, stock, buying and suppliers, double-entry accounts and 28 reports. There are also extras such as lay-bys, job cards, serial numbers, stock loans and recipes. See all features.

Can my staff have their own accounts?

Yes. Each staff member signs in with their own account, you choose what they're allowed to do, and the activity log shows who did what.

Can I export reports to Excel?

Yes. Every report exports to a CSV file, which opens in Excel.

Your computer

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. NTmart Go doesn't need much computing power and runs smoothly on older and low-spec Windows PCs.

Does it need the internet?

No. NTmart Go works 100% offline, so you can keep selling even when the connection is down. On the Complete plan, M-Pesa, KRA and SMS need a connection to reach outside the shop.

What do I need to run it?

Just a Windows PC. You don't need an internet connection or any technical setup.

Printers and hardware

Which receipt printers does it work with?

All popular receipt printers, including Epson, EPOS and Xprinter.

Does it work with barcode scanners and weighing machines?

Other hardware, such as barcode scanners and weighing machines, needs further configuration. Tell us what you use when you ask for a quote.

Setup

Is installation complicated?

No. Run the installer, activate your licence and start selling. There's no technical configuration.

How do I activate my licence?

After you buy NTmart Go, you'll get your licence details. Enter them in NTmart Go to activate it. If you get stuck, contact us.

I already use NTmart POS. Do I need to move my data?

No. NTmart Go works with your existing NTmart data, and you sign in with your existing NTmart user accounts.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Ask your administrator to reset it, or contact support.

Price and licence

How much does it cost?

There are two lifetime licences, each paid once. Standard is KES 15,000: a full POS with sales, invoices and quotes, stock and accounting. Complete is KES 25,000 and adds M-Pesa, KRA and SMS. Compare plans.

What is the maintenance fee?

Maintenance is 33% of your licence price, paid yearly: KES 4,950 a year for Standard or KES 8,250 a year for Complete. It covers training, visits for diagnostics, and custom upgrades.

Are there monthly fees?

No. You pay once for the licence and it's yours for life. The only ongoing cost is yearly maintenance at 33% of the licence price.

Is deployment included in the price?

No. Deployment and implementation are quoted separately, based on your location. If your PC needs preparing first, for example drivers, an operating system or supporting software, the quote includes those costs too.

How do I buy it?

Contact us with your location and the hardware you use, and we'll send you a quote.
