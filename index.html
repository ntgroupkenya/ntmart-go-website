What's inside

Everything a shop runs on

The modules ported so far cover the register, stock, buying, selling on account, accounts and reports.

POS register

Price tiers, minimum prices, fractional selling, split payments, held sales, custom items, voids, loyalty points and cash drawer sessions.

Thermal receipts

Raw ESC/POS printing on 80mm and 58mm printers, with four receipt formats, your logo and barcodes.

Stock control

Product editor, adjustments, stock takes, transfers between locations, product groups and price lists.

Buying

Suppliers, purchases, purchase orders, returns, and supplier payments with allocations and cheques.

Selling on account

Clients, invoices, quotations, credit notes, payments received, cheques, contra, and client and supplier statements.

Accounts & reports

Chart of accounts, journals, expenses and incomes, bank lines, VAT withholding, staff commission and 28 reports with CSV export.

Exact money

Every amount and quantity uses exact decimals. Multi-step writes run in one transaction and are safe to retry.

Team & settings

Your existing user accounts, team permissions, an activity log, payment methods and shop settings.

Extras

Lay-bys, job cards, serial numbers and warranty claims, stock loans, and recipes with production.

Roadmap

Where the Go edition is today

The Go edition is moving over module by module. It stays a preview, used next to the PHP edition, until licensing and release are done.

  1. Done

    1 · Foundation

    Database connection, sign-in with your existing users (with lockout and audit log), main window, catalogue browser.

  2. Done

    2 · POS register & receipts

    Pricing and tax, sales with split payments, stock deduction, ledger postings, held sales, voids, loyalty, cash drawer sessions, eTIMS queueing and ESC/POS receipts. Receipt QR codes are still to come.

  3. Core done

    3 · Stock

    Editor, adjustments, stock take, transfers, locations, groups and price lists. Bulk editing and CSV import are next.

  4. Done

    4 · Buying

    Purchases, purchase orders, returns, and supplier payments with cheques. Purchase printing is next.

  5. Done

    5 · Selling on account

    Invoices, quotations, credit notes, payments received and statements. Invoice printing and PDF are next.

  6. Done

    6 · Accounts & reports

    Chart of accounts, journals, expenses, VAT withholding, commission and 28 reports. The balance sheet matches the PHP edition to the cent.

  7. In progress

    7 · Integrations & extras

    Team accounts, settings, activity log and all five Extras are done. Next: PIN sign-in, backup and restore, then KRA eTIMS live send, M-Pesa STK push, SMS, WhatsApp and email.

  8. Planned

    8 · Licensing & release

    Licensing, an installer, an offline mode with bundled MariaDB, and self-update. After this the Go edition can replace the PHP edition at a shop.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to move my data?

No. The Go edition connects to the MySQL/MariaDB database NTmart already uses and works with the same tables.

Can I keep using the PHP edition?

Yes. Both editions can run at the same time on the same database. You can use the Go app for the screens that are ready and PHP for everything else.

Can shops use it in production yet?

Not yet. The Go edition is a preview until Phase 8 (licensing, installer and self-update) is finished. Until then it is for testing alongside the PHP edition.

How do you know the numbers are right?

Each module is checked against the PHP edition: the same action runs through both and the rows they write are compared. Totals and VAT match to six decimal places, receipts are byte-identical, and every client statement closes on its balance.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows PC and access to your NTmart MySQL/MariaDB server. On first run the app asks for the server details and saves them for next time.

Does it support KRA eTIMS and M-Pesa?

Sales are already queued for eTIMS, and the PHP edition sends them for now. The live eTIMS send and M-Pesa STK push are part of Phase 7.

Want to try the preview?

We're testing the Go edition alongside NTmart PHP. Talk to the NTmart team about getting it set up on a test copy of your database.
