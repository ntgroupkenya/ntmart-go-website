One system

Everything your shop runs on, in one place

i.

A quick, simple till

Scan or search, take cash or M-Pesa, split a bill, hold a sale and come back to it. Price levels, loyalty points and voids are all built in.

ii.

Stock you can trust

Every sale and purchase updates stock straight away. Run stock takes, move goods between branches and store rooms, and see what you have, where.

iii.

Books that balance

Sales, purchases, expenses and payments post to your accounts as you work. Pull up profit, VAT and balance sheet reports any time.

iv.

Sell on credit

Issue invoices and quotations, take part payments and send clear statements. Always know who owes you and how much.

v.

Buy from suppliers

Raise purchase orders, receive goods, return items and pay suppliers by cash, bank or cheque, with a full record of what you owe.

vi.

Control who does what

Give each cashier and manager their own login and only the permissions they need. The activity log shows who did what, and when.

Questions

Good to know

What do I need?

A Windows PC at each till. A barcode scanner, a cash drawer and an 80mm or 58mm thermal receipt printer are recommended.

Does it work with M-Pesa and KRA eTIMS?

Yes. You can record M-Pesa payments at the till today, and sales are prepared for KRA eTIMS. Direct M-Pesa STK push at the till and sending to eTIMS straight from the Go edition are on the way.

Can I run more than one branch?

Yes. You can keep stock in several branches and store rooms and transfer goods between them.

When can I get it?

NTmart POS Go is in final testing. Book a demo to see it in action and to hear when it can be installed at your shop.
