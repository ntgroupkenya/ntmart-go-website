Features

The whole shop, in one app

NTmart POS Go covers the till, the stock room, your suppliers, your credit customers and your books.

The till

  • Barcode scanning and quick product search
  • Cash, M-Pesa and your own payment methods
  • Split one bill across several payments
  • Sell by weight or part quantities
  • Retail, wholesale and custom price levels
  • Minimum prices and no-discount items
  • Hold a sale and come back to it
  • One-off custom items
  • Voids, with stock put back automatically
  • Customer loyalty points
  • Cash drawer sessions and end-of-shift cash-up

Receipts

  • Prints straight to thermal receipt printers
  • 80mm and 58mm paper
  • Four receipt layouts to choose from
  • Your logo and shop details
  • Barcodes on receipts

Stock

  • Add and edit products, groups and price lists
  • Stock adjustments
  • Stock takes
  • Several branches and store rooms
  • Transfers between locations
  • Full history of every stock movement

Buying

  • Supplier records
  • Purchase orders (LPOs), converted to purchases in one step
  • Receive goods and pay now or later
  • Returns to suppliers
  • Supplier payments by cash, bank or cheque
  • Supplier statements

Selling on credit

  • Client records with credit balances
  • Quotations, converted to invoices in one step
  • Invoices, with pay-now or pay-later
  • Credit notes
  • Payments received, part payments and refunds
  • Cheques: record, clear or mark as bounced
  • Client statements

Accounts & reports

  • Chart of accounts and journals
  • Expenses and other income
  • Bank statement lines
  • VAT withholding
  • Staff sales commission
  • 28 ready-made reports, including profit and the balance sheet
  • Export any report to a spreadsheet (CSV)

Staff & security

  • A separate login for every staff member
  • Pick exactly what each person can do
  • Accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords
  • Activity log of who did what, and when
  • Payment methods and shop settings

Extras

  • Lay-by: customers pay over time, then collect
  • Job cards for repairs and service work
  • Serial numbers and warranty claims
  • Stock loans to customers
  • Recipes and production runs

Under the hood

Built to be dependable

i.

Exact money

Every amount is worked out to the exact cent, never rounded along the way, so your totals and VAT always add up.

ii.

Nothing half-saved

A sale saves completely or not at all. A power cut or network drop can't leave half a sale in your books.

iii.

Fast on ordinary PCs

NTmart POS Go is a native Windows app, so it opens quickly and stays quick on everyday till hardware.
