Privacy

Privacy policy

How NTmart handles information on this website and in NTmart Go.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by DECODE IT Solutions, part of NT Group by Nemsey Traders Ltd ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us.

Information from this website

When you use the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address and message, plus your phone number and business name if you give them. We use them only to reply to you and to handle your request.

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Icons are loaded from jsDelivr, which receives your IP address as part of serving those files.

Data in NTmart Go

NTmart Go works 100% offline. Your business data, including products, users and sales, stays on your own computer and is not sent to us.

We only see your shop's data if you give us access, for example during a support session, and only for as long as needed.

Sharing and storage

We don't sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and email, and when the law requires it.

We keep contact form messages only as long as we need them to deal with your request and our business records.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you
  • correct it or delete it
  • stop using it for a particular purpose

To make a request, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
