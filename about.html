Our story

Less screen, more selling

Many businesses don't need the latest look or a long list of features. They need a system that opens when the shop opens, records every sale, and keeps the stock and accounts straight.

NTmart Go is that system. It's a plain, fast Windows program that covers sales, invoices and quotes, stock and accounting. It's simple to install with nothing to configure, works 100% offline, and runs smoothly on the computers businesses already have.

It comes from the team behind NTmart POS and works with your existing NTmart data, so NTmart users can switch without moving anything.
